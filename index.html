MaxHealthing

MaxHealthing is a personal, single-user training planner. It is not offered to the public, and only its owner signs in to it.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

  • With the owner's permission, it uses only these Google permissions for the owner's own account: Google Calendar events (calendar.events) to read the owner's training sessions and add the owner's own training events; Gmail send (gmail.send) to send the owner's own MaxHealthing reminder emails; and Gmail read (gmail.readonly) only to find the owner's own "MaxHealthing stop" emails so a reminder can be turned off. It also uses the owner's sign-in email (openid, email) to check that the right account is signed in.
  • Data is stored on the owner's own computer. The owner can open the app from the owner's own phone or another device through a private link that needs a secret key; that link is carried over an encrypted connection (Tailscale) to the owner's computer, and without the key it shows nothing.
  • Nothing is sold, shared with anyone, or used for advertising, and no one else reads it.
  • MaxHealthing's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
