MaxHealthing

MaxHealthing is a personal, single-user training planner. It is not offered to the public, and only its owner signs in to it.

Privacy policy

  • With the owner's permission, it reads and writes only the owner's own Google Calendar events and sends and reads only the owner's own MaxHealthing emails through Gmail.
  • All data stays on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with anyone, or used for advertising.
  • The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Terms

This app is for the owner's personal use only and is provided as is, with no warranty.
